# PRETRUWs - Prediscovery Truncated Universe and Worlds

**Date:** 31 Oct 2026
**Status:** v0.3 framework. Not a Hydra evaler.
**Conductor:** Dehydrus
**Product:** Evalathan, [github.com/pedramgoshATgmail/EVALATHAN](https://github.com/pedramgoshATgmail/EVALATHAN)

PRETRUWs (Prediscovery Truncated Universe and Worlds) is the physical-world evaluation framework. It holds the LuDaTs (Limited-universe Data Test sets) methodology family. Hydra still conducts the sixteen software evalers. Dehydrus conducts the seven world evalers. The two rooms do not mix scores.

## 1. Hierarchy (these words are not synonyms)

From the house vocabulary (paradigm, framework, method, methodology, mandate, protocol) and the same stack people already use for Agile / Scrum / Extreme Programming / MoSCoW / ASD (Adaptive Software Development):

Braidware, from the SEISMO glossary, is the post-4IR term for agentics, software, and hardware as one unit (often sandboxed). PRETRUWs worlds are Braidware exams: the physical side (optic, island, assay) only works with the fitted agentic / LLM / SLM (Small Language Model) stack that can act inside that world.

## 2. Third world (Industry 4.0), after Highsmith's second world

[Jim Highsmith, 1997](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Adaptive_software_development), *Messy, Exciting, and Anxiety-Ridden: Adaptive Software Development*, called the second world **unpredictable, nonlinear, and fast**. PRETRUWs takes that to a third world (Industry 4.0 / Braidware):

| Highsmith second world | PRETRUWs third world |
|------------------------|----------------------|
| Unpredictable | Multi-predictable (more than one live forecast, including a multiverse reading and an AI that can hold several forecasts at once) |
| Nonlinear | Bilinear (linear and nonlinear together) and supralinear (above linear - the slot where quantum computing is argued to fit) |
| Fast | Hyperfast / instancy-nearest (the Instancy principle: the aim of tech is a speed so high it stops being speed and becomes instant - already visible in transformer-style inference) |
| Nondeterminism | Polydeterminism (also called hallucinations and truncations): many determinations, not "no determination") |

This is doctrine, not a score. It does not replace Highsmith. It does not award a number.

## 3. Why LuDaTs is not Trajectly

Trajectly reads software / agentic traces (intangibles). LuDaTs locks a physical or part-tangible world (an optic, a field year, a lab table). Putting LuDaTs on Trajectly's exam shelf mixed those rooms. v0.3 moves LuDaTs under PRETRUWs and Dehydrus.

## 4. What each kind of thing is

- **Conductor:** the orchestrator who calls every evaler in one family and writes the report. Hydra and Dehydrus are conductors. A conductor is an engine of routing, not a utility, and not one more evaler.
- **Router:** the job inside a conductor (which cell runs, in what order, what prior cells another cell may see).
- **Engine:** a scoring body that emits cells or a headline. Hydra, Dehydrus, each evaler, and later HabTwin's scorer are engines.
- **Agent / agentic:** a unit that judges or acts. The sixteen and the seven are agentic evalers. Plugins (LLM, RAG 3.0) that a world needs are agentic boltons, not extra Hydra cells.
- **Utility:** a deterministic transform with no gate (the PRD line, the HabTwin filename line).
