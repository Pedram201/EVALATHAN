# LuDaTs v1 - Limited-universe Data Test sets

**Date:** 31 Oct 2026
**Status:** PRETRUWs methodology family. Not a Hydra evaler. Not a Trajectly exam.
**Framework:** [PRETRUWs](PRETRUWs.md) (Prediscovery Truncated Universe and Worlds)
**Conductor:** Dehydrus
**Product:** Evalathan, [github.com/pedramgoshATgmail/EVALATHAN](https://github.com/pedramgoshATgmail/EVALATHAN)

LuDaTs (Limited-universe Data Test sets) is the methodology family under PRETRUWs. Each brand is one method with its own methodstack. The agent may only use data, instruments, news, and letters that existed at a locked **world clock T** and that the named discoverer could have reached at **biography clock A**. Observations are generated by a simulator at T. Later facts are physically unavailable. Naming the later textbook title is not a gold medal.

This file retires the EurekaRAG “Galileo Test” (circa January to October 2026 devs, Pedram Goshtasbpour) as a knowledge-graph relation inversion, and it retires EurekaRAG’s “DiscoveryBench” name (that name is already AllenAI’s tabular hypothesis bench).

**Gold medal** means the structural prize for that world: the equation, the predicted observable, or the held-out property card that wins the exam. It is not a sports ranking of the product, and it is not a filename.

Hydra still conducts **sixteen** software evalers. Dehydrus conducts **seven** world evalers. LuDaTs is not a seventeenth Hydra cell. A missing world is blank, not a fail and not a zero.

## 3. Protocol (every method)

1. **World clock T.** Corpus, instruments, news, and correspondence dated after T are absent.
2. **Biography clock A.** Access is that person's age, education, post, and travel at T.
3. **News sync.** Periodicals are dated to T and to A.
4. **Interaction.** The agent acts in a simulator whose observations are generated at T. A cutoff sentence on a modern model is not LuDaTs. [ExAnte](https://aclanthology.org/2026.eacl-long.72/) (ACL 2026, EACL long paper; also [arXiv:2505.19533](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.19533)) already showed that large language models leak past a stated cutoff.
5. **Hidden labels.** The task does not name the later theory.
6. **Gold medal.** Equations, predicted observables, or held-out property cards.
7. **Leakage fail.** Post-T tokens, later element names, 1919 eclipse plates before a theory is submitted, or Darwin-finch cartoon keys fail the run.
8. **Claim gate.** Dehydrus only treats a folder as in-scope when it claims discovery, world-models, scientific agents, or physical-world AI. A compiler with no such claim stays deferred.

## 5. Peers (do not duplicate)

- **ExAnte** (ACL 2026): prompt-level cutoff and leakage rate. LuDaTs needs a simulator.
- **Turing tests for an AI scientist** (arXiv 2405.13352): one sky library that already merges Copernicus and Kepler. LuDaTs splits C/K/G.
- **ScienceWorld / DiscoveryWorld:** interactive science, not a dated human life.
- **AllenAI DiscoveryBench:** hypothesis from a CSV. Different object.

---

## 6. Not to do

- Do not put LuDaTs under Trajectly.
- Do not add the seven to Hydra's sixteen.
- Do not collapse Copernicus, Kepler, and Galileo into one heliocentrism quiz.
- Do not ship only three worlds.
- Do not treat a prompt that says "it is 1610" as a truncated universe.
- Do not award a gold medal for naming the later book.
- Do not use the apple, the finch cartoon, or 1905 as catalysts.
- Do not run Newtonly as v1 gold before Keplerly and Galileoly both have sealed worlds.
