Past, current, and next. This is the product roadmap, not a diary.

## Past - v0.2

Hydra conducts sixteen software and agentic evalers on a local folder. The laptop scan grades only what is on disk. A blank is not a fail and not a zero. The sixteen stay sixteen. Public coding exams (SWE-bench, PRDBench, Ragas catalogs) sit on Trajectly's exam shelf and are not run.

## Current - v0.3

First of two 4IR (Fourth Industrial Revolution) eval paradigms.

- Manifesto direction: *Until Artificial Intelligence Independently Eurekas on its own* (ASD-shaped paper, after [Jim Highsmith's Adaptive Software Development](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Adaptive_software_development)).
- Framework: **PRETRUWs** (Prediscovery Truncated Universe and Worlds).
- Methodology family: **LuDaTs** (Limited-universe Data Test sets) - seven world methods, each with its own methodstack.
- Conductor / router: **Dehydrus**. Window title DEHYDRUS. Command `evalathan dehydrus`. He is not an eighteenth Hydra score.
- Full desk ships all seven brands: Copernly, Keplerly, Galileoly, Darwinly, Mendly, Einsteinly, Newtonly. Newtonly is on the desk and deferred to v2 until Keplerly and Galileoly both show a sealed world.
- v0.3 names a sealed world file. It does not pretend a prompt cutoff is 1610. ExAnte (ACL 2026 / EACL) showed large language models leak past a stated cutoff.
- HabTwin is named on the Dehydrus report (`No HabTwin lab trace` or the file list). It is not a Trajectly exam and not an eighth LuDaTs cell.
